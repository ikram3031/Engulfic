### [E05] 2026-08-17: Fixed Product Cards Alignment, Carousel Controls, HTML Tagline & /shop/ URLs
- **Issue:** Product card taglines displayed raw HTML `<p>` tags, cards had uneven heights, the carousel lacked desktop navigation arrows and showed a scrollbar, and product URL links used `/product/:id` instead of `/shop/:id`.
- **Fix:**
  - Stripped HTML tags from the product tagline using Regex in `transformProduct.js`.
  - Wrapped the entire product card inside a single `/shop/:id` link in `ProductCard.jsx` and stopped click propagation on interactive children (wishlist/size buttons).
  - Maintained even card content height by applying a `min-h-[44px]` container for swatches/sizing.
  - Added desktop left/right navigation controls and hid the scrollbar in `NewArrivalsSection.jsx`.
  - Registered `/shop/:id` path mapping to the `<Product>` component page in `App.jsx` and updated application links.
